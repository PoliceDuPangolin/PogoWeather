Understanding Fog

Best Weather for Darkrai in Pokémon GO

Darkrai is Dark type, so its target weather is Fog. Fog also boosts Ghost types.

Look for a possible Fog window

  1. Open the Darkrai search and inspect Now estimates.
  2. Try 24h to compare possible windows across the analyzed cities.
  3. Check the in-game icon before raiding: mist outside or in a weather app does not by itself confirm Fog in Pokémon GO.

What if no city matches?

No estimated match does not prove Fog is impossible to find; it covers only the places and period analyzed. Search again later or add a city if you know its coordinates.

Matching weather does not establish raid availability

PogoWeather estimates conditions; it does not guarantee that Darkrai is in raids or that a catch will succeed. Check announcements and available raids in Pokémon GO separately.

Sources: official boosted type chart; raid battles — Pokémon GO support.
