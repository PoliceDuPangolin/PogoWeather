About PogoWeather

PogoWeather is a fan-made project designed to help Pokémon GO players quickly find cities where certain Pokémon may be weather boosted.

The site works without user accounts. Custom cities stay in your browser. The main weather logic runs on the backend so the method is not fully exposed in public JavaScript.
