GO Fest Windy Weather Guide for Pokémon GO

Windy weather is one of the most important and most confusing weather conditions in Pokémon GO. It boosts Dragon, Flying and Psychic-type Pokémon, which makes it especially valuable during events featuring Rayquaza, Necrozma, Mewtwo, Latios, Latias or other important raid bosses.

Which Pokémon benefit from Windy weather?

Windy weather boosts Dragon, Flying and Psychic Pokémon. During GO Fest or other major events, this can make Windy one of the most searched weather categories. A Windy location can be useful for legendary raids, rare spawns and event planning.

Why Windy weather is hard to predict

Windy is difficult because Pokémon GO may not rely only on the current wind speed. The game can appear to follow forecast windows or previous forecast data. This means a city may show Windy in-game while a normal weather app shows Cloudy or Mostly Cloudy.

PogoWeather uses wind windows and forecast-based logic to estimate this condition. It is still an estimate, but it is more useful than checking only the wind speed at the exact current minute.

How to search for Windy weather

The easiest method is to search the Pokémon directly. For Rayquaza, search Rayquaza. For Necrozma, search Necrozma. PogoWeather will automatically target Windy weather because these Pokémon are boosted by types linked to Windy conditions.

GO Fest Windy weather checklist

  • Search the target Pokémon on PogoWeather.
  • Compare cities with Windy predictions.
  • Check confidence and weather details.
  • Verify the in-game weather when possible.
  • Recheck at the next hourly weather change.

Important note

Windy weather can be different between Pokémon GO and public weather apps. Treat the prediction as a guide, not a guarantee. If you find a mismatch, use the Contact page to send the city, time and weather details.
