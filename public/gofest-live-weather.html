Live event shortcut

GO Fest Live Weather Tracker

The Live Weather Tracker is designed for fast event decisions. Instead of starting with a long guide, start with the weather category you need, then open the Pokémon search directly.

Windy targets

Windy boosts Dragon, Flying and Psychic Pokémon. It is the key weather for Rayquaza, Necrozma, Mewtwo, Latios and Latias.

Check Rayquaza Windy weather

Rainy targets

Rainy boosts Water, Electric and Bug Pokémon. It is the key weather for Kyogre and many Water-type raid targets.

Check Kyogre Rainy weather

Clear targets

Clear weather boosts Fire, Grass and Ground Pokémon. It is the main condition for Groudon and other Ground-type targets.

Check Groudon Clear weather

Fog targets

Fog boosts Dark and Ghost Pokémon. It is rare, but very valuable for Darkrai and Giratina-style targets.

Check Darkrai Fog weather

Use it with city comparison

After opening a Pokémon search, compare several cities and read the confidence score. If the weather details look borderline, check another city. PogoWeather works best as a fast filter for likely boosted locations.
