What weather boosts Groudon?

The best weather for Groudon is Clear. In Pokémon GO, Clear weather boosts Fire, Grass and Ground type Pokémon. Since Groudon has the relevant typing, it can benefit from that weather when the game displays it on the map.

This is important because Pokémon GO weather is not just cosmetic. It affects boosted types, wild spawns and raid catches. A player looking for a stronger starting level should always check whether the raid boss is boosted by the current in-game weather.

Why weather boost matters for raids

For raids, weather boost mainly changes the catch level. A normal raid boss is usually caught at level 20, while a weather boosted raid boss is usually caught at level 25. IVs are still random, so weather boost does not guarantee a perfect Pokémon, but the higher level can still be useful.

This is especially helpful if you plan to power up the Pokémon. Starting five levels higher can save resources over time. During limited raid windows, GO Fest events or raid hours, players often prefer boosted catches because each pass can feel more valuable.

Why the game may not match your weather app

Pokémon GO weather can differ from real-world conditions and from public weather apps. The game appears to rely on forecast-style data and hourly weather blocks instead of instant live observations. Because of that, a city can show one condition in the game while another app displays something else.

This is very noticeable with conditions such as Windy, Cloudy and Fog. Windy weather may depend on forecasted wind during a specific window, not only the wind you feel outside. Cloudy and Partly Cloudy can also be difficult because different providers use different cloud-cover thresholds.
