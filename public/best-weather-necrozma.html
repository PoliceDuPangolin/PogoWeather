Forms and weather

Best Weather for Necrozma in Pokémon GO

For regular Necrozma, look for Windy. Necrozma is Psychic type, one of the types associated with that weather.

Do not confuse the fused boss and the encounter

Necrozma can fuse with Solgaleo or Lunala, changing its form and types. Official game support says that after a Fusion Raid, the base Pokémon is the encounter. When planning a catch of regular Necrozma, search for Necrozma and check the weather icon in the game.

Use PogoWeather for Necrozma

  1. Open the Necrozma search.
  2. Compare locations in Now mode or choose 24h to inspect estimated windows.
  3. Confirm both the in-game weather and raid availability. The tool does not read the game directly.

Why check the form?

Forms can have different names, types and effects. PogoWeather suggestions may not cover every form; if yours is missing, do not assume that another form’s result applies.
