GO Fest Weather Boost Hub

GO Fest and major raid events are perfect moments to use weather boost strategy. When many legendary or rare Pokémon are available, knowing the best weather for each target helps you decide what to search first.

This hub groups important Pokémon by their most useful weather boost. Use it as a quick starting point during event hours, then open the PogoWeather tool to compare cities.

How to use this during GO Fest

Pick the Pokémon you want, open its guide, then use the search tool. If you are hunting Rayquaza or Necrozma, prioritize Windy cities. For Kyogre, look for Rainy cities. For Groudon, Clear weather is usually the target.

During busy event windows, speed matters. This page helps you move directly from the Pokémon to the weather category without checking the full type chart every time.
