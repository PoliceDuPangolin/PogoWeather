Privacy

Privacy policy

PogoWeather does not require a user account and does not directly collect personal data through registration.

Custom cities

Custom cities you add are saved in your browser’s local storage. They are sent to the backend only during a search so the weather results can be calculated.

External APIs

The site uses external services for Pokémon and weather data. These services may receive normal technical request information such as IP address and query parameters.

Google Analytics and AdSense

The code includes placeholders for Google Analytics and Google AdSense, but they must be enabled manually. If those services are enabled, this policy should be updated with cookie, analytics, and advertising information.
