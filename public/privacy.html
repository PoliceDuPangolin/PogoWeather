Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-02

PogoWeather is a fan-made Pokémon GO weather boost tool. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the website and how that information may be used.

Information you provide

If you contact PogoWeather by email, the information you choose to send may be used to reply to your message, investigate a bug, understand a weather mismatch report or improve the service.

Weather searches and technical data

When you use the tool, PogoWeather processes search terms such as Pokémon names and city coordinates needed to estimate weather boost conditions. The website may also receive standard technical information such as browser type, approximate connection data, request time and error logs for security, debugging and performance monitoring.

Cookies and local storage

PogoWeather may use cookies or local storage for basic website functionality, such as remembering display preferences. If analytics or advertising services are enabled, those services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure performance, prevent abuse and improve the service.

Advertising and Google AdSense

PogoWeather may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense or other advertising partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and personalize ads depending on user settings and applicable consent rules.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this website and/or other websites. Users can manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings and browser privacy controls.

Consent for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If advertising cookies or personalized ads are enabled, users in regions where consent is required may be shown a consent message through a consent management solution. Until advertising is activated, some advertising-related features may not be present on the website.

Third-party services

PogoWeather may rely on third-party services for weather data, hosting, security, analytics, email routing or advertising. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact: contact@pogoweather.com
